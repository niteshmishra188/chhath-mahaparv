Eco-Friendly Tradition

Chhath Puja: Nature, Sun & Purity

Unlike most Hindu festivals, Chhath is completely non-idolatory. It directly worships Surya Dev (Sun God) and Chhathi Maiya (Shakti) along with rivers, ponds, and natural elements. It emphasizes zero pollution, sustainable bamboo crafts, and seasonal organic harvests.

✧ Thekua (The Holy Offering)

Prepared with absolute hygiene and purity using wheat, jaggery, and ghee, Thekua is the primary prasad offered to the Sun God and distributed as divine blessings.

💧 Ganga & Water Bodies

Standing in river water during sunset and sunrise allows devotees to absorb solar rays and thermal energy while expressing gratitude to water ecosystems.
